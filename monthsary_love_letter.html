Endless Chapters

My dearest love, as another month passes in our story, I find myself sitting here thinking about how completely wonderful my life has become since you walked into it. Even when the miles or busy days keep us apart for a little while, my heart never stops finding its way back to you.

I've been missing you extra lately—in the quiet moments in the morning when my first thought is your smile, and in the late evenings when all I want is to hold your hand and talk about everything and nothing. Distance or time away only proves one undeniable truth: you are my home, my comfort, and my absolute favorite person in this entire world.

Thank you for every single laugh we've shared, for the gentle way you understand my soul, and for loving me so purely. Every day with you feels like a gift I never knew I deserved. I cherish the memories we've built so far, and my heart beats with so much excitement for all the tomorrows we still have yet to write together.

Happy monthsary, my love. No matter where we are or what we face, I am yours, completely and endlessly.

Forever Yours,

With all my love & devotion
